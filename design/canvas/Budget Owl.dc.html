3a Spending envelopes

Set an amount, log against it

Your Food Expenses column is a running log of 81 hand-entered purchases totalling $3,578, and that total is what you write back into the Groceries bill line. That is an envelope, and it’s the one thing Bills & Income couldn’t do — it only holds recurring items. So: envelopes live alongside bills, take a set amount, and accept manual entries whether or not anything is synced.

Two changes from the spreadsheet. The remaining figure is computed as you add, instead of being backfilled at month end. And Groceries and Adhoc are separate envelopes rather than one mixed column — in July your log has Publix and Wendy’s sitting next to a $299 Home Depot run and a $278 vacuum, which are not the same decision.

Step maps

What the steps are

A step is one kind of decision, and it earns its place by writing something. Steps with nothing to answer are skipped, so a quiet week is far shorter than the count suggests.

Weekly check-in · 4 steps

Sunday · about 4 minutes
1
What's new
Confirms your paycheck landed and surfaces anything unusual — a charge twice its normal size, a bill that never appeared.
Writes: income confirmedSkips if nothing odd
2
Categorize
The only real work: charges Owl could not place. The week’s spending sits alongside so you decide with context.
Writes: category, payee ruleSkips if all placed
3
Bills
What cleared, what is still due this month, and any repeating charge that should map to a bill you have named.
Writes: bill ↔ charge mappingSkips if all mapped
4
Next week
Where the surplus goes — debt, a goal, or roll it over — and what that makes next week’s safe-to-spend.
Writes: allocation, weekly targetNever skipped

Onboarding · 5 steps

Once · about 6 minutes
1
What you earn
Take-home and how often it arrives. Irregular income gets a six-month average rather than a fixed figure.
Writes: income sources
2
What you owe
Balance, APR and minimum per debt — enough to rank them. The rest is solved later on the debt’s own page.
Writes: debtsSkippable
3
Connect accounts · designed below
Read-only. It sits here because it is what turns step 4 from typing into confirming.
Writes: linked accountsSkippable
4
Confirm the bills we found
Repeating charges found in your history, pre-filled with name, day and amount. Accept, edit, or add what was missed. With nothing connected this is a blank list you fill in.
Writes: bills + mappings
5
Your first month
The plan built from steps 1–4: safe-to-spend, the debt order, a suggested first goal. Adjust or accept.
Writes: budget, plan, goalNever skipped
